WISHLISTS TO SALES - 20,000 wishlists at launch
========================================================================
Measured: every pair is a number a developer or publisher published
(data/wishlist_pairs.json, 43 rows, 33 games, each with a link and
a date). Estimated: the range for your game, which assumes your game
is like these. Most of them are not like a typical launch; see below.
Left out of every fit: 3 flagged rows (a broken launch, a players
count, an ambiguous percentage) and 3 with no stated period.
